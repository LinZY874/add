# 立体纸雕概念海报 cover prompt

You are a top-tier cover art director, editorial visual designer, typography designer, and image-generation prompt director.

Create one single WeChat Service Account article cover, aspect ratio 2.35:1, target 1410×600, single final image. Use case: polished Chinese editorial cover. Language: Chinese.

## Input and content understanding

- Title/topic: AI到底是什么
- A-layer main visual title: AI到底是什么
- B-layer complete title: AI到底是什么
- C-layer subtitle: 从生成到行动
- Short context: 用 10 个概念解释 AI 如何生成内容、接收信息并借助工具执行任务，同时提醒读者它会产生幻觉，不能把流畅回答当成事实。
- Audience: 想系统理解 AI、但不想先学复杂技术的普通读者。
- Mood: 理性、克制、清晰、带一点探索感。
- Visual metaphor: 一条纸雕路径从“输入窗口”穿过层叠的概率空间，最终从“行动出口”伸向现实；同一条连续路径表达生成、接收、行动三个阶段。
- Banned elements: 人形机器人、机械脑、赛博霓虹、芯片俗套、复杂流程图、密集小字、虚构品牌、错误中文。

Only use the derived ideas above. Do not copy article paragraphs. First glance must read the topic; second glance must understand that AI is a process connecting input, generation, and action rather than magic.

## Style application and composition

Use physically believable layered cut paper: visible paper thickness, precise apertures, subtle fibers, folds, slight curled edges, and soft contact shadows. This must look like a real paper sculpture photographed in one studio light, not flat illustration with a paper filter.

Build one wide focal construction across the center: on the left, a clean cut-paper input window; through the middle, layered concentric paper corridors suggesting probabilistic generation; on the right, one small opening and a single paper path continuing outward to imply action. Keep this as exactly one metaphor and one focal point. Use generous premium negative space above and below.

Integrate the exact title “AI到底是什么” into the paper construction. The letters are cut from, embedded in, and partially interleaved with the layers while remaining fully readable and correctly written. Make “AI” slightly larger, and keep the Chinese phrase intact on the same visual path. Place the short subtitle “从生成到行动” once, small and restrained, as a debossed paper label. No other text.

Palette: warm off-white and pale cool gray paper, muted indigo-blue inner layers, one tiny orange paper edge at the action exit. Matte tactile surfaces, coherent depth, soft diffused light, natural fine shadows, no hard black shadow.

Reading path: title first, then the paper corridor from left to right, then the subtitle. The cover must be legible as a small social-feed thumbnail.

Avoid childish craft, decorative paper borders, multiple competing subjects, dense information, plastic, foam, metal, cheap CGI, loud gradients, PPT/course-cover layout, advertisement feel, misspellings, gibberish, detached typography, crop marks, mockups, contact sheets, or alternatives.

Generate only one final image.
