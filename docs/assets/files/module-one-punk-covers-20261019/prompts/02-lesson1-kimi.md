# kimi风格 cover prompt

You are a top-tier cover art director, editorial still-life photographer, typography designer, and image-generation prompt director.

Create one single WeChat Service Account article cover, aspect ratio 2.35:1, target 1410×600, single final image. Use case: Chinese editorial explainer cover. Language: Chinese.

## Input and content understanding

- Title/topic: AI到底是什么
- A-layer main visual title: AI到底是什么
- B-layer complete title: AI到底是什么
- C-layer subtitle: 10个概念，看懂AI
- Short context: 文章用 LLM、Token、思考强度、幻觉、Prompt、Context、RAG、Tool、Skill、Agent 十个概念，讲清 AI 从生成内容到接收信息，再到执行任务的完整链路。
- Audience: AI 初学者与想建立系统认知的职场读者。
- Mood: 冷静、可信、好奇、研究档案感。
- Visual subject: 少量真实研究物件构成“语言模型如何连接输入与行动”的俯视档案桌。
- Visual metaphor: 一枚半透明玻璃棱镜接收左侧的文字 Token 小块，经中心的三段轨道，把一个实体小工具推向右侧，表现输入、生成、行动。
- Banned elements: Kimi Logo、任何真实品牌 Logo、产品界面、纸张堆、文件堆、人物、机器人、复杂 UI、霓虹科技风、乱码。

## Style application and composition

Use top-down archival still-life photography on a large pale gray-green light-table. Soft diffused light, realistic contact shadows, true scale, clean surface, low saturation, and generous negative space. This is a photographed working research table, not illustration, 3D render, or floating product stage.

Arrange one coherent horizontal object system with few objects only: at left, four small ivory ceramic tiles carrying abstract dot-and-line marks that suggest tokens but contain no readable text; at center, one clear glass prism over three slim parallel metal rails; at right, one precise stainless-steel drafting tool or compact mechanical gripper touching a small cobalt-blue block. The spatial relationship must clearly read as information entering, being transformed, and becoming action. Every object serves the topic; do not fill the table.

Reserve a pristine title zone in the center-left negative space. Render the exact title “AI到底是什么” once in sharp, modern Chinese editorial typography, large, dark charcoal, completely accurate and unobstructed. Add the subtitle “10个概念，看懂AI” once beneath it in smaller neutral sans serif. No additional labels, fake archive numbers, or decorative English.

Palette: pale mint-gray tabletop, ivory ceramic, clear glass, brushed aluminum, warm wood accent, charcoal type, one restrained cobalt-blue object. Materials must show real glass transparency, brushed metal, ceramic matte texture, and natural micro-scratches without dirtiness.

Reading path: title, then token tiles → prism/rails → tool block, then subtitle. Preserve wide breathing room and social-thumbnail legibility.

Avoid copied official layouts, Kimi marks, fixed archive templates, meaningless decoration, excessive papers, clutter, neon, cyberpunk, cheap futurism, complex UI, glow, blurred title, wrong Chinese, dense microtype, advertisement feel, PPT/course cover, grid, contact sheet, or multiple options.

Generate only one final image.
