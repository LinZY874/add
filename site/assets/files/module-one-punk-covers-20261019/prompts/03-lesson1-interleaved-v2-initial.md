# 超大标题图文穿插 cover prompt

You are a top-tier cover art director, editorial visual designer, typography designer, and image-generation prompt director.

Create one single WeChat Service Account article cover, aspect ratio 2.35:1, target 1410×600, single final image. Use case: high-impact Chinese editorial article cover. Language: Chinese.

## Input and content understanding

- Title/topic: AI到底是什么
- A-layer main visual title: AI到底是什么
- B-layer complete title: AI到底是什么
- C-layer subtitle: 生成 · 接收 · 行动
- Short context: 文章通过 10 个基础概念拆解 AI，说明它如何生成回答、使用当次上下文与外部资料，并通过工具、技能和智能体把回答变成行动。
- Audience: 对 AI 好奇的普通用户与职场读者。
- Mood: 现代、理性、直接、有探索张力。
- Visual subject: 一个单一、高识别度的“开口黑盒”装置，左侧吸入文字碎片，右侧伸出一只真实但无人物身份的工作手套夹持工具；装置整体轮廓清楚。
- Visual metaphor: AI 不是人形大脑，而是一个把输入转成输出并可连接行动的系统。
- Banned elements: 机器人、机械头、铬面人像、雕塑头像、未来假人、复杂面板、多主体、错字、乱码。

## Style application and composition

Use one central subject only: a matte-black rectangular “black box” with an open slit, photographed or rendered as a believable physical editorial object. From the left edge, a restrained stream of small white token tiles enters; from the right, one simple gloved hand/tool extension emerges, creating a strong clean silhouette. The subject occupies the central band but leaves obvious breathing room.

The exact title “AI到底是什么” is enormous, bold, geometric sans serif, and spans most of the landscape canvas. Create explicit depth layers inside the same title: some strokes sit behind the black box, the box occludes parts of them; other strokes cross in front of the box. Show at least two visible front/back order switches within the title, with both “subject over type” and “type over subject” in the core overlap zone. All seven Chinese characters and “AI” must remain legible, accurate, and recognizable; do not hide critical strokes.

Place the subtitle “生成 · 接收 · 行动” once in a small clean area, away from the title overlap. No other text. Use a disciplined palette of warm off-white, matte black, cool gray, and one restrained electric-blue accent on the output tool. Minimal thin editorial rules may be used, never a dashboard.

Reading path: enormous title, then black-box subject and its left-to-right transformation, then subtitle. The result should feel like a premium magazine or exhibition campaign and stay readable in a social feed.

Avoid whole-line translucent type, random overlap, mechanical top-background/bottom-foreground splits, text simply pasted over image, blocked characters, multiple unrelated subjects, noun-by-noun illustrations, chrome people, robots, PPT/e-commerce/feed-ad aesthetics, crowded text, gibberish, grids, contact sheets, or alternatives.

Generate only one final image.
