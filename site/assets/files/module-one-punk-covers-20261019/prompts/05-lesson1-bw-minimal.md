# 黑白极简概念 cover prompt

You are a top-tier cover art director, modernist editorial designer, typography designer, and image-generation prompt director.

Create one single WeChat Service Account article cover, aspect ratio 2.35:1, target 1410×600, single final image. Use case: conceptual Chinese editorial cover. Language: Chinese.

## Input and content understanding

- Title/topic: AI到底是什么
- A-layer main visual title: AI到底是什么
- B-layer complete title: AI到底是什么
- C-layer subtitle: 生成 / 接收 / 行动
- Short context: 文章用十个概念解释 AI 的三个层面：生成内容、接收当次信息，以及借助工具与智能体执行任务；流畅回答也可能是幻觉。
- Audience: 想快速建立 AI 基础认知的读者。
- Mood: 理性、冷峻、克制、带一丝警醒。
- Visual subject and metaphor: 巨型标题本身成为一条从“输入门”通向“行动出口”的建筑结构；一个微缩人物站在标题内部的路径分叉处，提醒读者 AI 是系统，不是神谕。
- Banned elements: 机器人、芯片、大脑图标、彩色科技背景、装饰插画、空洞大字、复杂信息图、错字乱码。

## Style application and composition

Use black, white, and gray editorial restraint, a precise modular grid, sharp alignment, large negative space, and light paper grain. Make the exact title “AI到底是什么” enormous and fully readable; the thick modern Chinese characters form the architecture of the whole image rather than sitting beside an illustration.

Embed only three metaphor anchors in the typography: a small rectangular doorway cut into the left side of the “A”; one continuous narrow path passing through the interior negative spaces of the title; and a tiny human figure at the right-side opening facing a single small orange light node. These anchors must grow from the title structure and visually imply input, transformation, and action. Keep the person truly tiny and secondary.

Place the subtitle “生成 / 接收 / 行动” once as sparse modernist microtype aligned to the grid, but still readable. No labels, years, account IDs, or fake editorial filler. Use mostly warm white, ink black, and two grays; the orange node occupies less than 1% of the image.

Reading path: title first, then doorway and path, then the tiny figure and orange node, then subtitle. Preserve high contrast and small-thumbnail legibility.

Avoid ordinary advertising, cheap commercial illustration, colorful treatment, decorative symbols, a big-type cover with no metaphor, over-distorted characters, heavy textures, unrelated objects, PPT/course cover, e-commerce feel, long article text, grids used as decoration, contact sheets, or alternatives.

Generate only one final image.
